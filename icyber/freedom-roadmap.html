Before you start

Hey, I'm Liliane. 👋

You work hard for your money. But is it creating the life you're working so hard for?

You have a demanding career. Bills to pay. People who depend on you. Responsibilities that don't simply disappear when your workday ends.

You're ambitious. You earn a respectable income. Yet somehow, despite everything you do, you don't always feel as financially secure or independent as you thought you would.

You might wonder where your salary goes each month. Whether you're saving enough. How to manage debt, build wealth or create additional income without sacrificing the little free time you have left.

And underneath all those questions is something much more important:

How do I build a financially secure future without missing out on the people and moments that matter most today?

I know that feeling. I started my career as a flight attendant, working toward my dream of becoming a pilot, until COVID changed those plans. I rebuilt my professional path in cybersecurity consulting, a demanding, traditionally male-dominated industry.

But even as my career progressed and my income grew, financial security didn't automatically follow. I was earning more, yet I wasn't intentional enough about what I kept, where it went, or what it could help me build. I eventually paid off €20,000 in debt, not by first increasing my income, but by understanding my finances and making deliberate changes.

That experience changed how I think about money. Financial independence isn't simply about earning more. It's about understanding what you have, making intentional decisions, building resilience, growing your money over time, and creating options that give you more control over your life.

That's why I created the TRUST Sheet, and this roadmap that takes you beyond it. No judgment. No unrealistic promises. No complicated jargon. Just a practical path for making your money work toward the life you actually want.

Is this for you?

Who this is for

  • You're building a demanding career, but your financial progress doesn't always reflect the effort you're putting in.
  • You're balancing work, family or caregiving, and you need a financial system that works with your limited time, not another task that overwhelms you.
  • You earn a reasonable or even substantial income, yet want more clarity about what you keep, spend, save and owe.
  • You want to move beyond paycheck to paycheck, build resilience and make more informed decisions about growing your wealth.
  • You're interested in additional income streams, but want a strong foundation before taking unnecessary risks.
  • You want financial independence for a reason: more flexibility, more choices and more meaningful time with the people you love.

This isn't for anyone looking for overnight wealth or guaranteed returns. It's for people ready to build lasting habits and make deliberate progress.

The method · KEEP + TRUST

KEEP = Know your Earnings to Escape your Paycheck. Your salary can be the foundation of your financial future, but it doesn't have to define your limits.

TTrack Know your numbers: income, expenses and what you actually keep.
RReclaim Find where money slips away and redirect it toward what matters to you.
UUnderstand See your position clearly and decide from facts, not assumptions.
SSave Build resilience, set priorities and prepare for your future.
TTime See how money and time connect, and why independence is really about choices.

Knowing your numbers is the beginning, not the goal. The TRUST Sheet gives you clarity. This roadmap takes you from there through debt, wealth building and additional income, all the way to more time. The TRUST Tracker (included) does the maths and tells you which Freedom Level you're on. Do the stages in order: each one makes the next one safe.

Where you are now

The Freedom Ladder

Freedom isn't one finish line. It's seven levels, and each one gives you more room to breathe. Your TRUST Tracker calculates your level automatically on the KEEP dashboard. You only ever need to work on the next rung.

Level 0Overwhelmed

More goes out than comes in. Every month ends in the red or on the credit card. Work on: Stage 1.

Level 1Stable

What you keep is €0 or more. No more sliding backwards. Work on: starter buffer.

Level 2Buffered

One month of needs saved. A broken washing machine is an annoyance, not a crisis. Work on: debt.

Level 3Debt-free

Consumer debt (cards, overdraft, buy-now-pay-later) is gone. That interest is now yours. Work on: full fund.

Level 4Secure

A full emergency fund of 3–6 months of needs. You could survive a job loss or a care emergency. Work on: investing and one stream.

Level 5Flexible

Extra income covers at least 25% of your needs. You could drop overtime or ask for a shorter week. Work on: time.

Level 6Free to choose

Extra income covers all of your needs. Work becomes a choice you make on your own terms.

How to fit this into a full life

Your rhythm: small, regular, forgiving

You don't need free weekends. You need a fixed slot that survives a hard week. Pick yours now and put it in your calendar as a recurring appointment with yourself.

Every week · 15 minutes

The weekly money check

  1. Open the KEEP dashboard. Look at one number: what you keep.
  2. Add any new costs or income to T · Track.
  3. Move this week's saving or debt payment.
  4. Do the next step from your current stage.
Every month · 30 minutes

The monthly review

  1. Copy the KEEP numbers into Monthly Review.
  2. Check your Freedom Level. Did it move?
  3. Write one win, however small.
  4. Choose the next month's single focus.
For heavy weeks

The 2-minute minimum

A hospital appointment, a deadline, a sick child, a week when caring takes everything you have. On those weeks, open the tracker, look at what you keep, and close it again. That still counts. The plan is designed to pause without breaking. Every stage page has its own heavy-week version.

Stage 1Weeks 1–4Goal: Level 1

See your money

Pillar 2 · Financial literacy & TRUST: Track, Reclaim, Understand

You can't build wealth on numbers you avoid looking at. This stage is about clarity, not cutting everything you enjoy. Most people find €50–150 a month they didn't know they were losing.

  1. Do the 2-hour setupDownload last month's bank and card statements. Book two hours, make a coffee, open T · Track.
  2. List every euro inNet salary, benefits and allowances, any side income. Monthly amounts; divide yearly ones by 12.
  3. Sort every euro outMark each cost Need or Want. Care costs, childcare and debt minimums are Needs. Be honest, not harsh.
  4. Find your baselineYour Needs total is what your life really costs. It becomes your freedom number later.
  5. Hunt the leaksIn R · Reclaim, list every subscription, fee and contract. Decide Keep, Cut, Reduce or Renegotiate.
  6. Make one callPhone, internet, insurance: ask for the current tariff or a loyalty discount. One call often saves €10–20 a month.
  7. Know your real hourly valueIn U · Understand, add contract hours, unpaid overtime and commute. That's what one hour of your life is worth.
  8. Give reclaimed money a jobSet up a standing transfer for your reclaimed amount on payday, before it can disappear.

Heavy-week version

Skip the full list. Write down only your five biggest costs and cancel one subscription you forgot you had.

In your tracker

T · Track, R · Reclaim, U · Understand. Done when the KEEP dashboard shows your keep rate.

10-minute quick win

Search your banking app for the word “subscription” or “abo” and cancel the first one you don't use.

Stage 2Months 2–5Goal: Level 2 → 3

Build your safety net

Pillar 2 · TRUST: Save, and Pillar 3 · Debt

When you look after other people, a surprise bill doesn't just cost money. It costs sleep. A small buffer first, then debt, is the order that protects you fastest.

  1. Open a separate buffer accountNot your everyday account. Easy to reach in a day or two, hard to spend from on impulse.
  2. Save one month of needsYour starter buffer. Until it's full, it gets every reclaimed euro and your first-hour transfer.
  3. List every debtIn U · Understand: balance, interest rate, minimum payment. The tracker shows the monthly cost of each one.
  4. Attack Priority 1Pay minimums on everything, then put every extra euro on the highest interest rate. The tracker ranks them for you.
  5. Stop new debtRemove saved cards from shopping sites. Turn off buy-now-pay-later. Use the buffer for emergencies instead.
  6. Roll payments forwardWhen one debt is gone, its whole payment moves to the next. Don't let it fade back into spending.
  7. Create goal potsChristmas, birthdays, car repairs, care costs: predictable “surprises” get their own small monthly amount in S · Save.
  8. Celebrate Level 3Debt-free is a huge milestone. Mark the date. Do something small and free with someone you love.

Heavy-week version

Keep the automatic transfers running and do nothing else. Automation is what keeps going when you can't.

In your tracker

S · Save (buffer, goal pots) and U · Understand (priority, months to clear, interest per year).

10-minute quick win

Look at “Interest you pay / year” on the KEEP dashboard. Write that number on a sticky note. That's what Stage 2 wins back.

Stage 3From month 2, ongoingRuns alongside every level

Grow your career capital

Pillar 1 · Career & cybersecurity

For most professionals the job is the biggest money machine they'll ever own. A €300 monthly raise takes no extra hours each week. That's more than most side hustles pay in their first year. Protect and grow it first.

  1. Know your market valueCheck salary reports and job ads for your role and city. Write down a realistic range, not a dream number.
  2. Keep a win fileOne note, updated every Friday: results, numbers, praise, problems you solved. It writes your review for you.
  3. Book the conversationAsk for your salary review 6–8 weeks before budgets are set, not on the day itself.
  4. Ask for more than moneyFlexible hours, remote days, training budget, a certification paid for. For carers, flexibility can be worth more than cash.
  5. Pick one skill with leverageOne course or certification a year that your market pays for. In cybersecurity: cloud security, governance, an entry certification.
  6. Make yourself visibleOne LinkedIn post a month about what you learned. Recruiters and future clients find you without you job hunting.
  7. Build a quiet safety planAn updated CV and five people you could call. Career security is part of financial security.
  8. Log every raiseAdd it to Income Streams as “Career”. Then decide where it goes: buffer, debt or investing, before lifestyle grabs it.

Heavy-week version

Add one line to your win file. That's it. Five minutes on a Friday builds a strong case over a year.

In your tracker

Income Streams (type: Career). Update your net salary on T · Track when the raise lands.

10-minute quick win

Start your win file now. Write down three things you did well in the last three months, with a number for each.

Stage 4Months 5–9Goal: Level 4

Secure it and start building wealth

Pillar 3 · Wealth building & investing education

First, finish the emergency fund. Then start learning how long-term investing works. This page teaches principles. It does not recommend any product, fund or provider.

  1. Finish your full fund3 months of needs, or 6 if you're a carer, a single earner or self-employed. Set the months in S · Save.
  2. Check what your employer offersWorkplace pension schemes and employer contributions are part of your pay. Find out what you're entitled to.
  3. Learn the core ideasDiversification, time horizon, compound growth, inflation, risk and volatility. Learn them before you buy anything.
  4. Know your time horizonMoney you need within 5 years doesn't belong in the stock market. Long-term investing is for long-term money.
  5. Watch the costsFees look tiny and compound massively. Always ask: what do I pay per year, in total, in percent?
  6. Choose a risk you can sleep withCould you watch your investment fall 30% and not sell? If not, take less risk. Your nerves are part of the plan.
  7. Automate, then leave itA fixed monthly amount, on payday, reviewed once a year. Checking daily helps nobody.
  8. Check who you're dealing withUse only regulated providers and advisers (in Germany, check the BaFin register). Guaranteed high returns are a red flag.

Add one income stream

Pillar 4 · Multiple income streams

Not five streams at once. One, tested for 90 days, in the 2–4 hours a week you can truly protect. Choose by what fits your time and energy, not by what's trending.

StreamTime to first €Hours it needsGrows without youGood fit if you…
Freelance / consulting●●●●●●●●●have a skill people already pay your employer for
UGC●●●●●●●●●can film short videos in small pockets of time
Digital products●●●●●●●●●solve one problem well and can write it down
YouTube / content●●●●●●●●●enjoy teaching and can be patient for a year
Brand deals & affiliate●●●●●●●●●already have an engaged audience, even a small one

●●● = fast / a lot / strongly. Rough guide only; your results depend on your skills, niche and time.

  1. Check the rules firstRead your employment contract on side work and conflicts of interest. Check how to register and pay tax on side income where you live.
  2. Pick one streamUse the table. Choose what you could start this month with the skills and hours you have now.
  3. Protect two blocksTwo fixed slots of 1–2 hours a week. Take them from scrolling or TV, never from sleep or family first.
  4. Run a 90-day testOne clear offer, shown to real people. Measure euros, not likes. Decide at day 90: keep, change or stop.
  5. Track € per hourIncome Streams calculates it. Under your job's hourly value? Make sure it's buying you something: skills, an asset or future freedom.
  6. Give side income a jobDecide the split up front, for example: part for tax, part to investing, part to your time goal.

Heavy-week version

Cancel the block, don't feel guilty, and write down one idea for next week. Paused isn't failed.

In your tracker

Income Streams: status, hours, € per hour, share of needs covered. Level 5 lights up at 25%.

Stage 6Month 12 onwardGoal: Level 6

Buy back your time

Pillar 5 · Time freedom & real life

This is what all the other stages are for. Money becomes time: fewer late nights, a day each week that belongs to your family, being there for the appointments that matter.

  1. Map your real weekIn T · Time, fill in the 168 hours as they are now, including care, admin and recovery.
  2. Draw your goal weekSame table, the week you want. How many more family hours? How many fewer work hours?
  3. Price the gapThe tracker shows what the work hours you want back are worth per year. That's your next income target.
  4. Take the free wins firstStop unpaid overtime, batch errands, ask a sibling for a fixed care day. Many hours cost nothing to get back.
  5. Negotiate flexibilityRemote days, compressed hours, a 4-day week, carer's leave or a sabbatical. Your Stage 3 win file is your evidence.
  6. Step down in stagesReduce hours only when extra income covers the difference for 6 months in a row and your full fund is intact.
  7. Protect what you gainedPut family time in your calendar like a client meeting. Freed hours fill up fast if nobody guards them.
  8. Reach Level 6, your wayExtra income covers your needs. Keep working, cut back, change direction. The difference is that now it's a choice.

Heavy-week version

Protect one hour this week for someone you love. Phone off. That is the whole point of the plan.

In your tracker

T · Time: unplanned hours, family time gained per year, value of the work hours you want back.

10-minute quick win

Look at “Family time gained / year” in T · Time. Write down what you'll do with the first of those hours.

What usually goes wrong

Five traps for busy people, and the fix for each

Trap

Waiting for a calm month. Busy people never get one. The plan never starts.

Fix

Start with the 2-minute minimum. Momentum first, perfection never.

Trap

Investing before you have a buffer. The first emergency forces you to sell at the worst time.

Fix

Follow the ladder. Levels 2–4 come before long-term investing.

Trap

Five side hustles at once. Nothing gets enough hours to work, and you burn out.

Fix

One stream, one 90-day test, one number to watch.

Trap

Lifestyle creep. The raise or first side income vanishes into nicer everyday spending.

Fix

Give every new euro a job the day it arrives: buffer, debt, investing or time.

Trap

Trading family time for “future freedom”. You build the life and miss the people it was for.

Fix

Family hours are planned first, side hours second. T · Time keeps you honest.
