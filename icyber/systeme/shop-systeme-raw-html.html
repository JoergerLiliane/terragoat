Why I built this

Hey, I'm Liliane.

I'm a full-time cybersecurity consultant at a Big Four firm, working in the financial sector. Alongside my 9–5, I support my mum, who is 83 and receives care at home after her cerebral haemorrhage.

In my 20s I took out loans for my study fees, made financial decisions I'd make very differently today, traded without really understanding it, and even fell for an online scam. I paid off €30,000 of debt, not by first earning more, but by understanding my finances and making deliberate changes.

Most financial advice told me to quit my job, hustle harder or get rich fast. None of it was made for someone with a demanding career and a mum who needs them. So I decided to build the plan I wish I'd had, step by step.

Questions

Before you buy

How much time do I need?

About 2 hours to set up, then 15 minutes a week. Later stages use 2–4 protected hours a week. Every step has a heavy-week version for the weeks when life takes everything.

Does it work with Google Sheets?

Yes. The tracker is an Excel file that also opens in Google Sheets (File → Import) and Numbers.

Will you tell me what to invest in?

No. The roadmap teaches how investing works: time, risk, fees and red flags. It doesn't recommend specific products, so you can make informed decisions yourself or ask a regulated adviser better questions.

I'm over 35. Is it too late?

No. The roadmap shows exactly why the best time to start was earlier and the second best is now. Starting at 35 still more than doubles what you pay in over 30 years (illustration at 5% a year).

How do I receive it?

Instantly after purchase: the roadmap as a PDF and the tracker as a spreadsheet file.
