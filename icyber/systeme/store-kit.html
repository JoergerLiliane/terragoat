ReviewPut the 7 stages at the centre of everything

Right now the card sells four separate things: a roadmap, a tracker with a technical name, "raise scripts" and a "90-day income test". A buyer can't tell how they connect, and you noticed the same thing. The fix is simple: the 7 stages are the product. The tracker and every script or template should belong to a stage, so the offer reads as one system.

Two things I'd change in the stages

Stage 1 and 2 overlap. "Understand how your 9–5 fuels your wealth" and "Money mindset: know your salary & the numbers" both talk about mindset. I'd make stage 1 the mindset stage and stage 2 the numbers stage: 1 · Money mindset: your 9–5 is your wealth engine and 2 · Know your salary & your numbers.

Debt before the safety net is risky. If someone throws every spare euro at debt with no buffer, the first broken washing machine goes straight back on the credit card. Most guidance suggests a small starter buffer first. Keep your order, but open stage 3 with "first save a starter buffer of one month of needs", and build the full safety net in stage 4.

Decided: "TRUST Tracker Pro"

The paid tracker is now called TRUST Tracker Pro everywhere: on the card, in the store and on the bundle image. The free version stays the TRUST Sheet, so free vs paid is instantly clear.

Is 50 pages realistic?Yes, if every page has a job

50 pages is realistic and justifies a regular price of €59. A buyer values 50 useful pages; they resent 50 padded ones. So plan it per stage: 6 pages × 7 stages = 42, plus the opening and the plan around them.

The 3 extras on the cardWhat they are, and where they belong

All three already exist in the roadmap and tracker I built for you. They just aren't explained on the card:

  • TRUST Tracker, Freedom Edition is the paid Excel / Google Sheets file: tabs for Track, Reclaim, Understand, Save and Time, plus Income Streams, a KEEP dashboard with your keep rate and next step, and a monthly review. It's the bigger version of your free TRUST Sheet.
  • Raise scripts & templates are in the career stage: how to prepare and run a salary conversation, a weekly "win file" to collect proof of your results, plus a script for calling providers to cut your bills.
  • The 90-day income test is in the income-stream stage: a week-by-week plan to test one extra income stream in 2–4 hours a week, then decide with real numbers whether to keep it.

Instead of listing them as random bonuses, give every stage its own tool. Then the extras stop looking like filler and become proof that each stage is practical:
