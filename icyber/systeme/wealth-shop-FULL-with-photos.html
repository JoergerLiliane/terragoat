Why I built this

Hey, I'm Liliane.

I'm a full-time cybersecurity consultant at a Big Four firm, working in the financial sector. Alongside my 9–5, I support my mum, who is 83 and receives care at home after her cerebral haemorrhage.

In my 20s I took out loans for my study fees, made financial decisions I'd make very differently today, traded without really understanding it, and even fell for an online scam. I paid off €30,000 of debt, not by first earning more, but by understanding my finances and making deliberate changes.

Most financial advice told me to quit my job, hustle harder or get rich fast. None of it was made for someone with a demanding career and a mum who needs them. So I decided to build the plan I wish I'd had, step by step.
